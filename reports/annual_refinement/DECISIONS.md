# Analysis decisions

All model development uses training members and the original seven validation members. Training-subset resampling evaluates cold and warm analogues before the nine fresh years can be scored. Atmospheric bases are fixed in those conditional uncertainty refits. The final model uses the full training cohort and is evaluated against separate ensemble members.

Compare the frozen public weights with each candidate under the same area, weather windows, input warming baseline and validation member-years. The ocean projection screen, the pointwise probabilistic ocean score, the conditional monthly wind error, and the variability of newly generated winds answer different questions. Report each separately.

For coupled-model selection, first examine the equal-weight mean of fractional CRPS changes across SST, mixed-layer depth and subsurface gradient, the relative physical RMSE of monthly steering given the actual background, and the absolute log error of pooled 5-, 10-, 15-day and full-month wind variances. Also inspect the individual fields, cold and warm tails, within-month centered variability, daily shear and the stability of the Gaussian dynamics. Prefer a modest rank when uncertainty or tradeoffs make extra parameters uninformative. Freeze the numerical recipe and weight hashes prior to scoring the new years. Report a research candidate, including the metrics that regress, if evidence is too weak to recommend changing the release.

The 27 years first used for ocean-only training form a second data ablation. Completing the corresponding atmospheric profiles and daily winds yields 57 paired training years per member, all inside the original training warming interval and disjoint from every validation or test year. The 30-year paired model with 57 ocean years remains a distinct experiment. The scoring members and both test panels stay fixed.

Shortlist one balanced fit and, if different, one fit favoring prediction of the conditional monthly wind per expanded cohort. The four primary axes have equal weight in the balanced score. A rank difference is resolved in favor of the smaller state when the composite differs by at most 0.003. The separate prediction shortlist weights the ocean score, monthly wind RMSE, total variance and within-month variance by 0.30, 0.40, 0.15 and 0.15. These are explicit modeling choices, not independent replications.

For each shortlisted parent, compare train-fitted weather calibration and ocean-only covariance adjustments. The latter operate in directions with identically zero transfer to monthly wind. Check this again on the actual exported system. Require the original 12 calendar months and seven validation members before resampling entire members and years. An interval is not a significance test over the many screened models. Select one physical candidate before releasing any independent metrics. No candidate owes its ranking to the September 2050 replay or to the 72 newly sealed member-years.

Target a primary decision by 12:10 UTC, leaving over two hours for both withheld panels, independent scenario replay, packaging and the final duration audit. Inspect the frozen candidate against the original warming groups, shear, predictability, uncertainty and calibration even when an individual metric regresses. A change in the public default is separate from making a verified research model available.

At 09:26 UTC we added a stronger spectral benchmark for wind-window statistics. The native TC package uses 15 harmonics, a 20-day period and spectral power exponent 3. Starting from that baseline, fit only the period (10–120 days) and power exponent (0–5) on the selected model's training members and years, separately for each calendar month. Minimize the equally weighted mean squared log errors of total and within-month window variances. The fitted climate background, instantaneous variance and number of harmonics are identical for matched Fourier and LIM. Publish this spectral sensitivity alongside the unchanged native driver. It is a weather control and cannot enter model selection. Paired intervals reuse whole members and years for every driver and month.

At 09:29 UTC we added a diagnostic for a structural difference between the two stochastic wind models. At each wet or dry atmospheric grid cell, compute the 4×4 lagged covariance matrix for daily u250, v250, u850 and v850 after removing that realization's monthly mean. Area-average within the North Atlantic basin and the tropical corridor. Compare lags 1, 3, 5, 10 and 15 days, using MPI component standard deviations for normalization. Report the complete matrix and its time-antisymmetric part. A scalar Fourier spectrum shared by the four independent factors has an exactly symmetric expectation at each lag, including after finite-month centering. A cross-component VAR may carry directional dependence. The experiment uses paired MPI member/year intervals, and remains outside finalist selection.

At 09:34 UTC the validation replay showed that a two-parameter Fourier spectrum can match the primary 15-day variance on this panel while missing the directional wind-component covariance. We therefore specified a training-only method experiment that refits the existing LIM retention and memory-feedback parameters using daily MPI winds. It compares three fixed weights on a directional-covariance training loss, retaining the model's warming amplitude, background and scalar variance targets. It reports held-out validation results independently of the previously specified primary model selection. No directional validation or test score changes that selection.
